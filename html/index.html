Austin Traffic Camera Images

This site hosts thumbnail images from traffic cameras operated by the City of Austin. Images are refreshed every 5 minutes and are available to the public.

Terms of use

Use of these images is subject to the City of Austin traffic camera terms of use. Please review them before using this data.

Fair use and rate limits

These images are provided as a free public resource. To keep the service available for everyone, requests are rate limited to 1000 requests per 5 minutes per client. Clients that exceed this limit will receive an error response and may be temporarily blocked.

Accessing images

Each camera's latest thumbnail is available at a URL based on its camera ID:

https://cctv.austinmobility.io/image/{camera_id}.jpg

For example: https://cctv.austinmobility.io/image/200.jpg

Camera locations and metadata

Camera IDs, locations, and other details are published in the Traffic Cameras dataset on the City of Austin Open Data Portal.
